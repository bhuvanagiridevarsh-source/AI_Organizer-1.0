## Sending notes

- The ⚡ personalization line is not optional. A template with a real specific first line outperforms a perfect template without one.
- Send from a personal-looking address (you@systemjanitor.com beats hello@), plain text, no images or tracking pixels — these audiences (especially lawyers) have aggressive spam filters and sharper eyes.
- Cold email is regulated: include a real mailing address and honor opt-outs (CAN-SPAM in the US; stricter rules like GDPR/PECR apply to EU/UK recipients — LinkedIn DMs are safer there). Not legal advice — worth 10 minutes of reading before a big send.
- One follow-up after ~4 days ("any chance the file mess resonated?") is fine. More than two total sends burns the well.
